The basics

What Is an Excel Amortization Schedule Template?

A structured spreadsheet for calculating and organizing loan repayment details over time.

An Excel amortization schedule template is a structured spreadsheet used to calculate and organize loan repayment details over time. It helps borrowers understand how each payment is divided between interest and principal while tracking the remaining loan balance throughout the repayment period.

A loan amortization spreadsheet typically includes:

  • Loan amount
  • Interest rate
  • Loan term
  • Monthly payment amount
  • Principal payment
  • Interest payment
  • Remaining balance
  • Payment schedule
  • Loan payoff summary

How it works

How Loan Amortization Works

The gradual process of paying off debt through scheduled payments — visualized month by month.

Loan amortization is the process of gradually paying off debt through scheduled payments over a fixed period.

Each loan payment usually includes:

  • Principal repayment
  • Interest charges
PHASE 01

At the beginning of the loan:

A larger portion goes toward interest
PHASE 02

As the loan balance decreases:

More of each payment goes toward the principal balance

An amortization schedule helps borrowers clearly visualize this repayment structure month by month.
